Nyx refraction modes

How a transmissive glTF material is refracted in Nyx's Forward mode, from its thickness and its sidedness. The shape of the mesh is never tested directly.

Decision

What each mode does

Thin
The background is seen straight through, at its real place. IOR only sets the reflection strength. No absorption, except in the fallback case, which absorbs over the thickness value.
Thick
The surface is treated as a flat slab. The exit point is computed from the thickness value and the light leaves in its original direction, so the background is shifted sideways. On a curved surface this gives a distorted image, not a lens.
Mesh
The light is refracted in, a ray finds the real exit face, and the light is refracted out with that face's normal. The thickness value only switches the mode on; the distance comes from the geometry.
